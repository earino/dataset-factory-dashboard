icfes-saber11-school-outcome-decline

investigating · CO 02:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Colombian Calendario A secondary school whose Saber 11 school-year T sitting results are published (individual results are published on a dated official calendar, e.g. sitting 2025: exam applied 2025-08-10, individual results published 2025-10-10), predict whether the school's mean global score (PUNT_GLOBAL, 0-500 scale, school-year T+1 sitting) falls below its school-year T mean by at least a fixed margin. Features are the school's own published history up to and including sitting T: per-subject school means and cohort sizes for sittings <= T, school registry attributes (calendar, naturaleza oficial/no oficial, genero, jornada, bilingue, area, DANE department/municipality codes), and cohort context shares from the publisher's own questionnaire fields (FAMI_*: estrato, parental education, household assets), all documented in the publisher's 51-column schema with descriptions.

What you predict

target definition

Binary label per (school, transition T->T+1): 1 if the school's mean PUNT_GLOBAL over its scored examinees in the Calendario A school-year T+1 sitting is lower than its school-year T mean by at least the fixed margin (margin or a percentile-rank variant chosen at construction; both measured feasible). Label source columns: PUNT_GLOBAL of sitting T+1 (and the sitting T+1 rows generally) - excluded from shipped features by construction (features use sittings <= T only). Measured base rates (schools with n>=10 examinees on both sides, server-side aggregates): any decline 52.42% / 20.66% / 62.54% / 51.04%; decline >=5 points 31.98% / 9.87% / 42.23% / 33.55%; decline >=10 points 16.08% / 4.45% / 24.01% / 19.69% for the four available Cal-A transitions 2014->15, 2015->16, 2016->17, 2019->20. Censoring: a school with no sitting-T results is not eligible (excluded, not imputed); a school with no T+1 sitting (closed/merged/moved to another calendar) is observed absence of an outcome and is excluded from the scored panel - construction must record the non-random-exit rate as part of the definition (school persistence measured: 9,025-10,952 of ~11.9-13.7k schools appear in adjacent sittings).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Prediction instant = the official publication date of the sitting's individual results (dated example, Colombia Time COT=UTC-05:00, no DST): sitting T = Calendario A school-year 2025, exam applied Sunday 2025-08-10 (ICFES calendar PDF 2025_Calendario11A_V03.pdf; ICFES news 2025-08-08 '640 mil personas presentaran las pruebas Saber 11 calendario A' este domingo 10 de agosto; some municipalities reprogrammed to 2025-08-31 per news 2025-08-12), individual results published Friday 2025-10-10 (calendar PDF row 'Publicacion de resultados individuales Saber 11 en pagina web: viernes 10 de octubre de 2025'). INSTANT = 2025-10-10 COT (hour not stated by the publisher; date-level instant). Feature event window closes at the instant: school aggregates for sittings <= T are published (with sitting T's results, ICFES also publishes its per-school aggregate reports - 'Otros resultados examen Saber 11': Clasificacion de Planteles, Reportes de Resultados Agregados). Eligibility at instant: school has >=10 scored examinees with valid PUNT_GLOBAL in sitting T (measured school counts per sitting: 10,701-12,778 at n>=10 of 11,906-13,748 raw). Target window = Calendario A school-year T+1 sitting: exam applied ~August T+1 (2026 Cal-A sitting: results-publication news dated 2026-09-22), results published ~Sept/Oct T+1 - the whole target window (exam, scoring, publication) falls ~11-13 months AFTER the instant; no part of it precedes the instant. AS-OF / VINTAGE POLICY (recorded limitation, not resolved): the open MICRODATA for a sitting is published on datos.gov.co with a long lag (measured: per-year 2020 datasets uploaded 2021-03-15; unified table's 2022 sitting appears at rowsUpdatedAt 2023-08-23), so the constructed panel is a CURRENT-VINTAGE RETROSPECTIVE panel: an operational system at the historical instant would have used ICFES's same-day aggregate reports, not the open microdata; no point-in-time operational claim is made for the microdata channel, and microdata revisions between snapshots are not excluded (the 'Resultados unicos' table is itself a publisher curation; ESTU_ESTADOINVESTIGACION is a publication-consent status, measured values 'PUBLICAR' 1,065,436 / 'VALIDEZ OFICINA JURIDICA' 452 for 20224). Label-definition caveat (measured): national sitting-level mean shifts between adjacent sittings are large (school-mean deltas: +8.35 (2015->2016), -2.97 (2016->2017), -8.83/-9.54 on Calendario B pairs) because ICFES equates scores per sitting, so an absolute-points decline label partially reflects national equating drift; construction must either define the label relative to the sitting's school-mean distribution (percentile-rank drop, equating-invariant) or ship sitting-level national aggregates as history features and state the conflation explicitly.
