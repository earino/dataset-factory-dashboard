The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a children's home registered in England, given everything published about it in Ofsted bulk vintages released on or before instant T (prior full/monitoring inspection history with dates and judgements, registration attributes, and its owning organisation's portfolio outcomes in the same vintage), will the home's next FULL inspection after T be rated below Good (Requires improvement to be good, or Inadequate)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label from the overall judgement of the home's next full inspection after T: 1 = 'Requires improvement to be good' or 'Inadequate' (old 2016-2017 wording 'Requires Improvement' mapped identically), 0 = 'Good' or 'Outstanding'. Source column: 'Overall Effectiveness' (quarterly MI files 2016-2023) / 'Overall experiences and progress of children and young people' (annual 2024+ files and SFR files); this column is the label source and is excluded from shipped features. Homes that deregister before their next full inspection are censored, not labelled 0. Population: provision type children's home (incl. short-break-only, multi-building; 'children' in provider type), Active or Suspended in the feature vintage, with at least one prior graded full inspection visible in vintages released <= T.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The documented GOV.UK release date of an Ofsted bulk vintage (Europe/London). Features may include only what a vintage released on or before that date contains: prior inspections, judgements and registry attributes. Per-event 'Inspection publication date' columns exist in the 2024+ products; pre-2024 availability is anchored to vintage release dates from the GOV.UK 'Updates to this page' log (e.g. the Dec-2019 quarterly file, released 13 Feb 2020, is the first bulk source of Sep-Dec 2019 inspection outcomes). The target inspection is dated strictly after T, so no part of the target window precedes the prediction instant.
