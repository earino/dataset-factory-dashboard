The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the moment a boil water advisory is issued for a public registered or municipal drinking water supply in Nova Scotia - known then: site name, county, facility type, reason for the report, calendar season, and the prior advisory history of the same site/county computed only from earlier advisories - will the advisory be lifted within 30 days?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: advisory lifted within N days of issuance, N=30 primary (N=14 secondary; both base rates measured below). Label source columns: 'Date Advisory Removed' and 'Length of Advisory in Days' - label-only, excluded from shipped features. Publisher documents the censoring semantics on the dataset page: 'Values are not provided for boil advisories that are currently in effect.' (blank removal date = active). Scored rows require a removal date at the pinned vintage; censored rows are counted per window and excluded from scored sets, not treated as observed non-lifts.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The advisory issue date (register column 'Date Advisory Issued', calendar day, America/Halifax). Every shipped feature must be a value fixed at or before that day: the issuing row's own site name, county, facility type, reason for the report, and derived calendar features; site/county history features may only use rows with an earlier issue date. The label window is the 30 days that follow the issue date. No part of the target window precedes the instant.
