The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a concentration notified to the European Commission under the EU Merger Regulation, using only case attributes fixed at notification (the parties as named in the case title, the case's NACE sector codes, the applicable regulation, and the notification date), will the Commission open an in-depth Phase II investigation (adopt an Art. 6(1)(c) decision) in this case?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the case record carries at least one decision of type 'Art. 6(1)(c)' (the decision by which the Commission initiates proceedings because of serious doubts - the Phase II opener, same article number under Reg 4064/89 and Reg 139/2004). Label source: the decisions[] block of the register record (decisionTypes, decisionAdoptionDate), which is excluded wholesale from shipped features. Measured: 309 positives / 10,123 eligible cases = 3.05%. Measured alternative recorded for construction: any-intervention union (Phase II opener OR Phase I remedies 'Art. 6(1)(b) with conditions & obligations') = 673 / 10,123 = 6.65% (remedies-only 367 = 3.63%).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The notification date of the case (caseNotificationDate; date-only precision, Brussels local). All shipped features are case attributes fixed when the file is registered: caseTitle (the merging parties, an immutable historical fact), caseSectors (NACE codes of the parties' activities; current-vintage register coding, see revision_note), caseRegulation (4064/89 or 139/2004), caseNotificationDate. The label event - an Art. 6(1)(c) decision opening Phase II - is adopted after notification: measured 308/309 openers strictly follow the notification date (302/309 within 2 months; single documented exception M.3333, see anomalies). Fields that can change after notification are excluded from features and recorded below. No feature's target window precedes the instant.
