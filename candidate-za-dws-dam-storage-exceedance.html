The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a listed South African dam, given the weekly storage series published through week W (DWS State of Reservoirs bulletin / NIWIS export), will that dam's storage fall below a per-dam drought threshold (e.g. % of full supply capacity, or absolute storage) at any point in the next 4 weeks? Prospective users: municipal water departments (restriction timing), irrigation boards and agri insurers (planting/coverage decisions), and the DWS's own weekly briefing cycle. A useful prediction supports earlier restriction and allocation decisions than the week-lag bulletin alone.

No official threshold table (per-dam drought/restriction levels) was obtainable this session; the threshold would be fixed from training data (as in other exceedance candidates) or from an obtainable official document.
