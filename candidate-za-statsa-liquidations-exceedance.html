The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the publication instant of the Stats SA P0043.1 'Statistics of liquidations' release for reference month M (published in the last week of month M+1, embargo lifting 14:30 SAST = 12:30 UTC; observed: 25 Mar 2026 for Feb issue, 27 May 2026 for Apr issue, 30 Sep 2026 14:30 for Aug issue, expected 28 Oct 2026 for Sep issue), will the number of liquidations in month M+2 for a given unit (companies/close-corporations x compulsory/voluntary x industry) exceed that unit's trailing 12-month median count over months M-11..M? Prospective users: credit and insurance exposure monitors, procurement/counterparty risk teams, and macro early-warning analysts, who get a ~1-month lead on industry-level insolvency pressure for provisioning and review decisions.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: count of liquidations for unit u in month M+2 (LIQ series value, 'Actual figures', number) > median of u's counts over months M-11..M (both operands from the same current-basis vintage; label source column = the unit's monthly count series itself, which is also the feature source - a row's shipped features are lagged values only, months <= M, never the target month's value). Alternative measured formulation: YoY direction (M+2 count > M-10 count). Trailing-median rows with median 0 (sparse series, all-zero window) are excluded as unlabelable; measured exclusion ~32% of unit-months under the 40-unit population.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Instant = the announced embargo lift of the P0043.1 monthly release for reference month M (date+time published in advance by Stats SA; observed publication dates all fall in the last week of month M+1 at 14:30 SAST, lag 25-34 days after month end). At that instant, the latest published reference month is M; month M+1 is partially elapsed (the instant falls inside it), so M+1 can be neither feature nor target; the target month M+2 begins after the instant (about 4 days after) and ends about 34 days after it. Features are the released series values for months <= M only (current-vintage policy; see measurements.restatement_2026 and measurements.intra_basis_revisions). Dated example (Africa/Johannesburg): instant 2026-10-28 14:30 (embargo of the Release 2026-09 issue); feature window 2000-01..2026-09 as released in that vintage (first-available version = the monthly release; values for months M-11..M used by the label's trailing median are the values published at or before the instant); eligibility cutoff = series values for months <= 2026-09 exist; prediction instant 2026-10-28 14:30; target window 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-30 (starts 3 days 9.5 hours after the instant; ends 33 days 9.5 hours after). No part of the target window precedes the instant.
