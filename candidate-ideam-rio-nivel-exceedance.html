The question

what a model is asked to predict

HYPOTHESIS under test: given a Colombian river station's hourly maximum-level telemetry through hour H (raw automatic-sensor readings, published openly on datos.gov.co), predict whether the water level will exceed the station's official alert threshold within the next 48 hours. A useful prediction feeds municipal early-warning (IDEAM/Carabineros/SNGRD protocols) with lead time for evacuation and road closures. The exact missing fact: an official per-station alert/danger threshold in machine-readable form - without it the label degrades to a self-referential percentile, which is a different (weaker) task.

What you predict

target definition

PROPOSED (blocked on threshold source): binary - station-hour (or station-day) level exceedance of the official alert threshold within (H, H+48]. Label source: valorobservado hourly maxima (cm/m) per station; the threshold would come from an official IDEAM table (NOT FOUND in the bounded probe: the station catalog cqmv-a99d has no threshold columns, and a catalog search for alert-level datasets returned nothing machine-readable). Fallback (weaker): per-station seasonal high-quantile exceedance - requires a known-event fixture (e.g. a documented 2021-2025 flood on a specific river) before that route may be probed further.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Telemetry is hourly and near-real-time on the live feed; the published dataset is one current table of raw unvalidated sensor values (publisher clause: 'los datos ... no han sido validados por el IDEAM'), and IDEAM documents a preliminary -> en revision -> definitivo lifecycle for its validated series (separate 'Niveles Horarios' dataset). UNRESOLVED: the as-of policy - current raw values may later be corrected, so features computed from today's table may differ from what the live feed showed at the time; construction must either use only the raw-feed table (accepting and measuring the revision effect) or obtain vintage information. This is the main open question besides the threshold.
