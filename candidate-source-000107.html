The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the first day of a community water system's next Lead and Copper Rule tap-sampling period, will that period's reported 90th-percentile lead concentration exceed the 15 µg/L action level?

What you predict

target definition

1 if the target monitoring period's PB90 record (lead 90th-percentile summary, SDWA_LCR_SAMPLES.CONTAMINANT_CODE = PB90) has SAMPLE_MEASURE > 0.015 mg/L, else 0. Target periods are restricted to SAMPLING_START_DATE <= 2023-12-31 so the 15 µg/L action level of 40 CFR 141.80(c)(2) (2021 revision, in force through the LCR Improvements transition) governs the label.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

First day, the SAMPLING_START_DATE, of the target PB90 monitoring period. Every prior lead or copper measurement, violation date and milestone date used is strictly before that date; current-vintage system attributes are time-invariant covariates.
