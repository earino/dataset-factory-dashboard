The question

what a model is asked to predict

No task formulated: the IMF's publishable products fall into two families, and both carry measured bench evidence of unsuitability. (a) The statistical catalogue (IFS, BOP, DOT, GFS, WEO, commodity prices, exchange rates, AREAER, Financial Data - 108 datasets on data.imf.org) is country-year/quarter macro panels: a level target is persistence-dominated and a change target's positives cluster into a few global shock periods, the exact family closed on measured evidence at oecd-sdmx-aggregate-family (class A) and itu-datahub-ict-panels (class B), with the same numbers mirrored by originating national offices and the WDI/FRED/Eurostat ecosystem. (b) The one row-level register - the lending-arrangements history (1,693 arrangements 1952-2025 per the IMF-derived IMF Monitor compilation; the IMF's own History of Lending Commitments pages publish facility, arrangement date, expiration, amount agreed/drawn/outstanding per member) - supports only a program-outcome task (completion/cancellation/interruption), the family measured twice at the World Bank sibling and dropped both times for identity-dominated labels with no headroom over a country rate-table (wb-ieg-project-outcome, wb-loan-disbursement-completion), here at a thinner population (~23 arrangements/year, clustered by member country) with an existing econometric literature on program interruption.
