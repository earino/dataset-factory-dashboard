The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an asylum corridor (country of origin O, country of asylum H) whose total government-procedure asylum applications in year D are at least 500 (persons, all application types summed), will that corridor's total government-procedure asylum applications in year D+1 be at least twice the year-D value? Annual grain, measured 2000-2025; a useful prediction enables humanitarian reception planning and resettlement budgeting ahead of the surge year rather than after it.

What you predict

target definition

Binary exceedance: label 1 iff the corridor-year (D+1, O, H) government-procedure persons total (sum of all application-type rows; a missing corridor row with the host still reporting counted as 0 applications) is >= 2 x the corridor-year (D, O, H) total. Label source: the same 'Number of Applications' column of the HDX CSV at year D+1; the year-D+1 value and everything derived from it are excluded from shipped features. Censoring: corridors whose host reports no G/P rows at all in D+1 (33 of 7,622 eligible rows, 0.43%) are excluded as unobserved targets, not counted as 0. Note what the target does NOT measure: temporary-protection registrations (e.g. Ukrainians in the EU from 2022) are not asylum applications and do not enter this stream.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Annual publication-lag NOWCAST - the accepted abs-visitor-arrivals-direction pattern, and it must not be described as a future-event forecast. The prediction instant is the publication of year-D complete annual data on the machine-fetchable channel: UNHCR's annual statistical cycle lands mid-year in D+1 (Global Trends), and the HDX distribution of this dataset was observed refreshing on 2025-11-05, 2026-02-25, 2026-06-11, 2026-06-19, 2026-06-22 and 2026-08-20, with the 2026-08-20 resource carrying complete data through 2025. At that instant every feature is a corridor-year total for years <= D. The target year D+1 has PARTIALLY ELAPSED at the instant (the 2026-08-20 instant lies inside target year 2026, which started 2026-01-01) but its value is entirely UNPUBLISHED: on this channel no D+1 value exists until ~mid-D+2, and for most non-EU hosts no public monthly corridor data exists at all (Eurostat monthly data covers EU hosts only and is outside this channel's shipped features). So the claim recorded here is value-unavailability at the instant, not that the target window follows the instant. A strictly-future variant (at the same instant, predict year D+2 >= 2x the last PUBLISHED year D) was considered and rejected at discovery: the doubling baseline must be published at the instant, so a strict future window forces a two-year-back comparison, a different label and different measured base rates - a different task. The HDX archive is revised in place (current-vintage), so construction pins the fetched snapshot by sha256 and states values as construction-time vintage - the same documented posture as abs-visitor-arrivals-direction. Dated example in notes.md.
