The question

what a model is asked to predict

A legislative proposition (Projeto de Lei, PEC, PLP, PDL, PRC, Requerimento and other documented types) is presented in the Brazilian Chamber of Deputies at a known date. At the presentation instant, given only presentation-time context - proposition type, the bill's own ementa (the abstract written at introduction), its author(s) and their party/faction, and the presentation calendar - will the proposition ultimately be transformed into legal norm (enacted) rather than archived or merged, by the close of its legislature?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the publisher's own situation vocabulary in the bulk CSV's ultimoStatus (current status at the file vintage; file and live API agree, measured 2026-10-23): positive = situation code 1140 'Transformado em Norma Jurídica' (CORRECTED 2026-10-23: the discovery sample's 'idSituacao 251' was a positional indexing defect - column 25 is ultimoStatus_idTipoTramitacao, not idSituacao; the header-name-keyed parse shows 1140 with a consistent code->description table, e.g. 923 Arquivada, 1230 Transformado em nova proposição, 937 Vetado totalmente). Terminal negative = Arquivada* codes plus the other settled non-enactment states (Vetado totalmente, Retirado pelo(a) Autor(a), Devolvida ao(à) Autor(a), Tramitação Finalizada, Enviada ao Arquivo). Merged rows (1230 'Transformado em nova proposição') are EXCLUDED from the scored population - the outcome is carried by the merged-into bill (decision recorded; share measured per window). Rows with a non-terminal status, and the 25-53% of rows (by year, measured) whose idSituacao is empty - the API confirms genuinely no tramitação on record, not a file gap - are unobserved targets, excluded and counted per window, never membership decisions on future data. Label source columns: all ultimoStatus_* fields and urnFinal, excluded from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant of presentation (dataApresentacao). Excluded from shipped features by documented timing reasoning: the publisher's indexação fields keywords and proposicoesTemas (assigned by the Chamber's indexing service after presentation - post-hoc, and the file documentation itself lists them beside 'informações sobre a tramitação mais recente'); all ultimoStatus_* fields (the current/last tramitação state - label source); regime as recorded in ultimoStatus (current regime, mutable after presentation); uriPropAnterior/Principal/Posterior (later-created links); urnFinal. Safe features: siglaTipo/codTipo/descricaoTipo, ementa text, dataApresentacao + calendar features, authors (from the separate proposicoesAutores bulk family), ano/numero.
