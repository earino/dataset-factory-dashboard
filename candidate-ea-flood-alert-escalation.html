ea-flood-alert-escalation

investigating · GB 13:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Flood Alert (or pre-2010 Flood Watch) issued by the Environment Agency for a target alert area P on day D, will any of the Flood Warning Areas whose register 'parent' is P receive a Flood Warning or Severe Flood Warning message during days D+1 through D+7, given only the warning-message history for P and its child areas through day D, the register attributes of P and its children, and the calendar?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label: 1 if any warning-level message (TYPE 'flood warning', 'severe flood warning', or their 'update' variants, case-normalised) for any warning area W with register parent(W) == P has issue date in [D+1, D+7]; else 0. Source columns: DATE, CODE, TYPE in the Historic Flood Warnings ODS joined to fws_tacode->parent in the Flood Warning Areas register (WFS, properties-only). Alert events eligible only when P has at least one child warning area (53,048 of 62,897 alert-level messages).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Day-grain: the day D on which the alert message was issued. The dataset's DATE column carries no time-of-day (measured: 0 of 85,982 dated rows), so the prediction instant is the alert's issue day and the label window starts at D+1 because a same-day child warning cannot be ordered against the alert (46.9% of [D,D+7]-window positives were same-day). Features must be knowable by end of day D: message history through D (inclusive), register attributes (current-vintage snapshot, measured static in practice), and calendar.
