The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the post-auction publication instant of a weekly Ghana Treasury/GOG tender (or Bank of Ghana bill tender), predict the next auction's marginal-rate direction and/or subscription coverage from the full published auction history. A useful prediction would support the same treasury/cash-management decision the weekly results exist for. The licence veto recorded below was established before any archive probing; no novelty claim is made in either direction.

What you predict

target definition

Direction of the next auction's accepted rate per tenor, and/or subscription coverage (bids tendered vs target amount). Label derivable from the published results pages. Not probed further: bare copyright veto.
