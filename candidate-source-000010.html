The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a single-union NLRB representation election that has been scheduled but not yet tallied, will a majority of valid ballots be cast for the union?

What you predict

target definition

union_win = 1 if 'Votes for Labor Union1' > 'Votes Against'; union_win = 0 if 'Votes Against' > 'Votes for Labor Union1'. Elections with equal votes, or with zero ballots counted, carry no majority and are dropped rather than labelled.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Election day, immediately before the ballots are counted (after the pre-election conference or direction of election has fixed the bargaining unit and the eligible-voter count). Date Filed, region, ballot type, unit description and unit location are all known strictly earlier than this instant.
