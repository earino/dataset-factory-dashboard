The question

what a model is asked to predict

A building fire has been reported and the first company is on scene. Will flame damage extend beyond the room of origin (to the floor, the building, or past the building), or will it be held to the room it started in?

What you predict

target definition

Binary, from fireincident.FIRE_SPRD (Structure Fire Module block J2, extent of flame damage): label 1 if FIRE_SPRD in {'3','4','5'} (confined to floor of origin, confined to building of origin, beyond building of origin); label 0 if FIRE_SPRD = '2' (confined to room of origin). Rows with blank, 'U', or any other value are dropped, not labelled 0 - fires confined to the object of origin carry no J2 code (they are flagged on Fire Module block D3), so the target is conditional on flame damage having already passed the object of origin.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At first arrival, using the ARRIVAL timestamp on the Basic Module, before fire control. Inputs are limited to attributes a size-up can establish: occupancy, structure type and status, building height and main-floor area, presence of detectors and of an automatic extinguishing system, alarm hour and weekday, dispatch-to-arrival interval, and reporting state. The current-vintage snapshot cannot prove each attribute was entered before the J2 assessment; that is a retrospective availability assumption.
