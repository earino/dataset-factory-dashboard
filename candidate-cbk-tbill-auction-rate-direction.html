The question

what a model is asked to predict

For the next weekly Kenya Treasury bill auction after the latest published results (auction N+1), will the 91-day tenor's weighted average interest rate of accepted bids exceed auction N's 91-day weighted average rate? (Same formulation extends to the 182- and 364-day tenors.)

What you predict

target definition

Binary direction label: auction N+1's 'Weighted Average Interest Rate of accepted bids' for the 91-day tenor > auction N's value for the same tenor. Label source columns: the per-auction result PDFs (one PDF per auction covering all tenors); both operands excludable from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The publication instant of auction N's dated results PDF on the CBK Treasury Bills page (auction 2705, dated 2026-10-26, was live on the page when fetched 2026-10-30, so the observed publication lag is at most ~4 days; not yet pinned to a precise hour). Features: all published auction results up to and including N; target: auction N+1's outcome, revealed when its results PDF is published. The auction calendar (weekly Monday auctions) is documented by CBK's own Auction Rules and Guidelines (2024, fetched via the same page).
