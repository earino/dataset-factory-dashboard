The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the TED publication of a contract notice (call for competition), will the linked award notice report that the procedure's lot received exactly one offer (single bid)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary single-bid label per procedure lot: NUMBER_OFFERS = 1 in the linked contract award notice (CAN) row for that lot, vs NUMBER_OFFERS > 1. Label source columns: CAN's NUMBER_OFFERS (plus the FUTURE_CAN_ID join key); both excluded from any shipped features. Pre-eForms CANs also carry NUMBER_TENDERS_SME / NUMBER_TENDERS_OTHER_EU / NUMBER_TENDERS_NON_EU breakdowns of the same total.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The TED publication instant of the contract notice (CN). The CN CSV export carries the buyer's dispatch date (DT_DISPATCH) and year; the exact publication date (PD) per notice is served by the anonymous TED Search API v3. Worked example in notes.md: CN 10-2023 dispatched 2022-12-28, published 2023-01-02+01:00 (Europe/Brussels); its linked award notice 371295-2023 was published 2023-06-21+02:00, i.e. the whole target window (award decision, offers received) follows the prediction instant.
