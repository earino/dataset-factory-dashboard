The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a long-term drinking water advisory (LTDWA, in place >12 months) on a First Nations public water system in effect at month M, will the advisory be lifted within the following N months, given only advisory and system attributes knowable at M (advisory type, region, population band, corrective-measure category, months already in effect)?

What you predict

target definition

Advisory lifted (lift date). The lift decision is made by a First Nations environmental public health officer after water-quality verification - a documented process, so the label semantics are sound. Measured blocker: the machine-readable export carries NO lift date. The 158 lifted-advisory records carry AdvisoryID, AdvisoryType, CategoryID, CommunityName, CommunityNum, WaterSystemName, DateSet, DateLTDWASet, DateExpected (a projected lift date), CorrectiveMeasure, LongPhase, PopulatioEstimated, RecommendedEPHO/RecommendedEPHODate/RecommendedEPHOIND, Region, coordinates - and no DateLifted. The export's own resource labels include 'AdvisoryLiftedDate' -> 'Date Advisory Lifted', so the display table intends the column, but the published data omits the value. Lift dates exist only in narrative HTML: the per-advisory tables for systems NOT financially supported by ISC (isc.gc.ca/eng/1516134315897) carry 'Date revoked (YYYY/MM/DD)', and lifts of ISC-funded advisories appear in news releases and the 'Recently lifted' list - reconstructable only by scraping prose, not from a machine-readable product.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Stateable in principle - a monthly status instant during each advisory. ISC republishes the advisory register continuously: the map page's own 'Download map data' button exports a machine-readable file with GenerateDate 2026-10-20. Not taken further: capacity and label-source availability failed first (see rejection_reason).
