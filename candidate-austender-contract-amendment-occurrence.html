ready · AU 17:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an Australian Government contract whose original Contract Notice is published on AusTender at instant T (the award-notice publication date, which follows the award itself within the ~42-day contract reporting window), will the contract receive its first published amendment notice (Contract Notice Type 'Amendment', CN ID suffix '-A<n>') within 12 months after T? Features are the published notice fields fixed at T: agency, supplier (name/ABN/location), committed value, UNSPSC category, procurement method, description text, planned start/end dates and derived duration, panel/SON/ATM linkage, consultancy and confidentiality flags.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: 1 if any Contract Notice with Parent Contract ID == this CN ID (equivalently CN ID == '<parent>-A<n>' per the publisher's documented convention) is published with Amendment Publish Date in (T, T+365d]; 0 otherwise. The label source columns are the parent/amendment linkage, Contract Notice Type and Amendment Publish Date - all excluded from shipped features (only parent-row fields plus strictly-earlier aggregates ship). Amendments of every published reason (value increase, end-date extension, supplier change, cancellation) count as events; the 'Amendment Reason' field allows construction to state and test reason-specific variants. Censoring: parents whose 12-month window extends past the data cutoff are excluded, not imputed (live-edge rows counted per window).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant is the parent Contract Notice's Publish Date (in-row, publisher-documented as 'the date the CN was published on AusTender'; the amendments-report channel carries it as a datetime with second precision, e.g. parent CN3439382 published 2017-07-07 15:48:21). Reporting follows the Commonwealth Procurement Rules: non-corporate entities must report contracts at or above $10,000 (inc. GST) and prescribed corporate entities at or above $400,000, within the ~42-day contract reporting window (Finance's statistics page states FY data is extracted after 42 days 'to allow for the 42-day contract reporting window'), so the award precedes the instant by at most ~6 weeks and every shipped feature is a published-notice field. The target window (T, T+365 days] begins strictly after the instant; amendment notices are separate later publication events with their own publish dates.
