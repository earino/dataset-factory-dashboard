The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a health claim submitted to EFSA under Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 - either a general-function claim (Art. 13.1) from the Member State list, or an application (Art. 13.5 / Art. 14) received via a national competent authority - will EFSA's NDA Panel conclude that a cause-and-effect relationship is established (favourable opinion), rather than not established (unfavourable)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: favourable vs unfavourable Panel conclusion on the claim. Label source: the published EFSA Journal opinion's conclusion ('a cause and effect relationship has [not] been established'), cross-checkable against the Commission's EU Register of Nutrition and Health Claims (authorised vs non-authorised status + reasons). Label source columns are excludable from shipped features; the register/opinion fields that carry the outcome are not features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

For Art. 13.5/14 applications: the instant EFSA receives/validates the application (published per the Transparency Regulation regime on OpenEFSA); the Panel 'shall' adopt its opinion within 5 months excluding stop-the-clock periods (EFSA guidance). For the Art. 13.1 batch: the compilation of the Member State claim list (4,637 main entries, published as the EFSA claims database, 2010) precedes the Panel's wave evaluations (2009-2011). Features are the submitted claim content only: claim wording, food/constituent, claimed effect, target group, conditions of use, article route, applicant identity (13.5/14).
