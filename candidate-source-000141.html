The question

what a model is asked to predict

A mining operation has just filed its first quarterly employment and production report. Will it report no work hours at all over the next two years?

What you predict

target definition

1 if total HOURS_WORKED summed across all subunits is zero in every one of the eight quarters immediately following the decision quarter, counting a quarter with no Form 7000-2 record as zero hours; 0 if any of those eight quarters has positive reported hours.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Close of the mine first quarter with positive total reported hours, as observable when MSHA posts the quarterly extract. The portal states the bulk files are refreshed weekly on Friday afternoons; the exact quarter-to-post lag is not verified here, so 45 days is used only as a conservative planning bound. Entry-quarter filing values are available then; current-vintage Mines inventory attributes are assumed low-volatility proxies rather than as-of-decision records.
