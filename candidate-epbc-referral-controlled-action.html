The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a development referred under the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (EPBC Act), at the instant the referral is validated and published on the EPBC Act Public Portal (with its title, proponent, industry type, location and primary jurisdiction visible), will the delegate's referral decision (due within 20 business days of validation) declare the action a controlled action (requiring formal assessment and approval) rather than 'not a controlled action' or 'not a controlled action - particular manner'? Prospective users: proponents and their environmental consultants (screening and budgeting for assessment before lodging), and oversight researchers studying what drives regulatory thresholds. A useful prediction prices assessment risk at referral time instead of waiting the ~4 weeks for the decision.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: 1 if the referral decision declares a controlled action (assessment approach determined on the project page; decision notice 'Controlled Action'), 0 for 'Not a controlled action' / 'particular manner' decisions. Label source = the per-project decision field/notice on the portal, excludable from shipped features (features are referral-time fields only). Population: all referrals in the portal's All-referrals list; still-open referrals at the data edge are censored, not imputed.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Instant = the referral's validation/publication on the EPBC Act Public Portal (per-project pages show a stage timeline Lodgement -> Validation -> Referral Decision -> Assessment -> Final Decision; the portal's list carries a 'Valid Date' column). Features fixed at that instant: project title, proponent name, industry type, location (address string and primary jurisdiction), and referral document metadata. The referral decision (the label) is published later on the same project page as a decision notice, within the statutory 20-business-day window - the outcome cannot exist at the instant.
