The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will a Certificate of Aircraft Registration issued to a U.S. civil aircraft be cancelled within two years of issuance, i.e. terminated before its first scheduled expiry — the registry-visible event produced by an ownership transfer to a buyer who registers separately, an export, a scrapping, a revocation, or an administrative cancellation?

What you predict

target definition

Binary. Build registration episodes by unioning the Master file (open episodes) and the Deregistered file (closed episodes), keyed on (N-number, serial number, aircraft manufacturer/model code, certificate issue date), dropping exact duplicate keys. Label = 1 if the episode has a Cancel Date on or before certificate_issue_date + 730 days; label = 0 if the episode is still open in the Master file at snapshot (no Cancel Date) or its Cancel Date falls after +730 days.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The certificate issue date recorded in the registry at day level, before any later cancellation. Only issuance-time attributes are used; no field dated after issuance enters the feature set.
