The question

what a model is asked to predict

FIXED 2026-11-07 (label sources measured, see notes s6.2): at the lodgement instant of a development application lodged through the SA PlanSA portal, predict whether the application will be decided within the statutory timeframe applicable to its assessment pathway under the PDI Act 2016 (primary; a per-application version of the statutory-timeframe KPI that SA/Vic/NSW publish only as aggregate percentages), with the per-consent decision outcome (refusal) as a secondary label. Prospective user: an applicant or their planning professional at/before lodgement, and councils forecasting assessment workload; a useful prediction supports pre-lodgement advice and resource allocation. Both labels derive from the register's per-application DecisionDetails (decision type / decision / decision date), measured available 2026-11-07 on a verified example (app 6154: 'Development Approval / Granted / 30 Apr 2021', lodged 1 Apr 2021) via a machine-readable detail endpoint - but that channel was also measured to degrade to hollow responses under sustained querying (notes s6.3), so label-row availability rate under a paced extraction is the last unmeasured input.

What you predict

target definition

FIXED family 2026-11-07; prevalence unmeasured. Primary label, binary: statutory-timeframe latency = decision date (DecisionDetails DD, e.g. '30 Apr 2021') minus lodged date exceeds the PDI Act assessment timeframe for the application's pathway. Pathway timeframes documented from the publisher's own 'Stages and timeframes' page (read 2026-11-07): deemed-to-satisfy 5 business days; performance-assessed 20 (no notification/referral), 50 (referral, assessment manager), 70 (public notification, panel/SCAP); restricted 95; building consent class 1/10 20 (+10 SCAP referral); land division 60; plus 5 business days verification up front and 2 business days to issue; AND a documented clock-SUSPENSION confounder (info requests, up to 12 weeks, clock restarts) that the register does not publish per application - the latency label must declare this as a limitation, and a published late decision is still needed as the known-event fixture. Secondary label, binary: refusal = per-consent DecisionDetails DRT in {Refused, Rejected} for the planning consent; 0 = granted/approved; Withdrawn/Lapsed/Cancelled and still-Under-Assessment rows are censored, not negatives (coarse statuses are backend-enforced: Status=Refused/Granted search POSTs return no rows while Status='Decision Made' returns 260,311 + rows, negative validated by positive control, notes s6.5). Population: PDI-era applications (lodged >= 31 Jul 2020) in the statewide register; the register also carries 73,464 DEV-act-era rows (measured, incl. 36,067 decided 2010-2019 and 26,532 decided 2000-2009) which are a different, state-referred population and are EXCLUDED from the frame. Both label source fields live in a JSON child separate from every lodgement-time feature, so the label sources are excludable from what ships.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At lodgement of the application. Features displayed by the register that are documented at that instant: description (free text, e.g. 'Commercial', 'Verandah', 'Outbuilding (domestic storage shed)'), Assessment Pathway (e.g. 'Accepted'), Elements (e.g. 'Internal fitout', Demolition/Land division/Telecom codes DM/LD/TF/TA/RDT), Council area, Decision authority type (Assessment Manager / Assessment Panel / Council / DAC / Governor / Independent Assessor / Minister / SCAP / State Planning Commission), Act (DEV/PDI), applicant name, property address and title. Post-lodgement fields to exclude from features: status, decision, decision date, notification outcome, and any post-hoc reclassification. Two timing questions remain unresolved: (1) whether the displayed Assessment Pathway/category is exactly the value assigned at lodgement or a later-revised value (needs the PDI Act guidance pages); (2) no decision DATE has yet been observed on a detail page (only the decision TYPE on the Assessment tab), which blocks candidate A's latency label. The register is a current-state service: it showed applications lodged 05/11/2026 by the 06/11/2026 ~03:55Z fetch, and historical vintages are not archived, so a task built from it is retrospective with an explicit as-of assumption; decisions may also change after the fact (status vocabulary contains 'Decision Under Appeal', 'Lapsed').
