investigating · AU 06:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

OPEN between two candidate labels, to be fixed by the decision-date probe (next_action 1-2). Both share the same population (DAs lodged through the SA planning portal from 31/07/2020) and the same prediction instant (lodgement). Candidate A (preferred, higher utility): will this application be decided within the statutory timeframe applicable to its assessment pathway under the Planning, Development and Infrastructure Act 2016 - a per-application version of the statutory-timeframe KPI that SA/Vic/NSW report only as aggregate percentages. Candidate B: will the decision be a refusal (status in {Refused, Rejected}), with Withdrawn/Lapsed/Cancelled censored rather than negative. Prospective user: an applicant or their planning professional at/before lodgement, and councils forecasting assessment workload; a useful prediction supports pre-lodgement advice and resource allocation.

What you predict

target definition

OPEN - pending the decision-date probe. Candidate A: binary label 1 = decision made more than the PDI Act statutory timeframe (working days, per pathway/category, clock-stop rules documented in the PDI (General) Regulations 2017 - rules not yet extracted; a published example decision is needed as a known-event fixture). Source columns: lodged date + decision date + pathway, decision date NOT YET CONFIRMED displayed per application. Candidate B: binary label 1 = decision status in {Refused, Rejected} (statuses documented in the register's own vocabulary: 'Refused' orange, 'This was refused.'; 'Rejected' orange, 'This was rejected.'); 0 = granted/approved; Withdrawn ('the applicant has withdrawn this application from assessment and no decision may be made'), Lapsed, Cancelled and still-Under-Assessment rows are censored and excluded from the label population - they are not negatives. Label source columns are excludable from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At lodgement of the application. Features displayed by the register that are documented at that instant: description (free text, e.g. 'Commercial', 'Verandah', 'Outbuilding (domestic storage shed)'), Assessment Pathway (e.g. 'Accepted'), Elements (e.g. 'Internal fitout', Demolition/Land division/Telecom codes DM/LD/TF/TA/RDT), Council area, Decision authority type (Assessment Manager / Assessment Panel / Council / DAC / Governor / Independent Assessor / Minister / SCAP / State Planning Commission), Act (DEV/PDI), applicant name, property address and title. Post-lodgement fields to exclude from features: status, decision, decision date, notification outcome, and any post-hoc reclassification. Two timing questions remain unresolved: (1) whether the displayed Assessment Pathway/category is exactly the value assigned at lodgement or a later-revised value (needs the PDI Act guidance pages); (2) no decision DATE has yet been observed on a detail page (only the decision TYPE on the Assessment tab), which blocks candidate A's latency label. The register is a current-state service: it showed applications lodged 05/11/2026 by the 06/11/2026 ~03:55Z fetch, and historical vintages are not archived, so a task built from it is retrospective with an explicit as-of assumption; decisions may also change after the fact (status vocabulary contains 'Decision Under Appeal', 'Lapsed').
