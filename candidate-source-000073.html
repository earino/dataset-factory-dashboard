The question

what a model is asked to predict

Given a large U.S. publicly owned treatment works' biosolids annual report for reporting year Y - its sludge volume, management-practice mix, pathogen class, treatment train, and per-pollutant monitoring results against Part 503 limits - will that facility be recorded with at least one Part 503 biosolids violation starting in the following twelve months?

What you predict

target definition

Binary. Label = 1 if the facility's NPDES ID (finalroot.npdesId_u1) has at least one row in biosolidsViolations.csv with violationStartDate in the half-open window [1 Oct Y+1, 1 Oct Y+2); else 0. Violation rows are matched on the facility NPDES ID (npdesId_u0), not through the year-(Y+1) report key, so no field of the outcome-year report can be pulled into features. Facilities with no violation record in the window are negatives.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

1 October of year Y+1, after the 19 February federal annual-report deadline and the September state filing wave; only reports with ReportingYear at or before Y and violation records with start dates before that date may be used.
