The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will an active U.S. public library service outlet (central library, branch, bookmobile, or books-by-mail point) that reported in survey year t be gone from the national Public Libraries Survey frame three survey years later?

What you predict

target definition

y = 1 if the outlet key (FSCSKEY, FSCS_SEQ) reported in year t is permanently gone: absent from the FY t+1 outlet file and also absent from FY t+2 and FY t+3, or present in FY t+1 only as a closed record coded STATSTRU='03'. y = 0 if the key is present in FY t+1 and either persists or returns (temporary closures coded 23/24 count as 0).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Close of the state fiscal year covered by survey year t, at the latest 31 December of year t. Only FY t outlet and administrative-entity fields are used; the shipped cohort_anchor_date is 31 December of year t, a conservative marker standing for the latest possible close of that reporting period, because the release carries no per-record period end date.
