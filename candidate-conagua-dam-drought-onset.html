conagua-dam-drought-onset

ready · MX 23:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Mexican dam in the CONAGUA SIH reservoir network (210 catalogued dams, 26 states) whose published stored volume VolumenAlm on the latest available day T is at or above the drought threshold (25 percent of the dam's reference capacity), will VolumenAlm fall below that threshold on at least one day in the 30 calendar days after T (a drought-onset event)? A useful prediction supports water-allocation planning, drought-monitoring inputs (the official Monitor de Sequia en Mexico uses reservoir availability as an index), and urban supply outlooks (Monterrey 2022, Tijuana 2019-2022, Falcon 2002 crises all crossed this threshold in this panel).

What you predict

target definition

Binary: 1 if VolumenAlm(Mm3) < threshold on at least one day with Fecha in (T, T+30] in the dam's SIH series, else 0. Label source column: VolumenAlm(Mm3) (the same column as the target variable), excluded from features by the Fecha <= T filter; no other column carries the answer. Threshold frame, two candidates measured in the sample: (a) discovery-time proxy = 25 percent of the dam's own observed maximum VolumenAlm (the SIH catalog carries no capacity column); (b) construction-time preferred frame = 25 percent of official capacity, published per dam in CONAGUA's weekly 'Almacenamiento de las Principales Presas del Pais' report (columns CAPACIDAD MUERTA / CAP. UTIL DISENO Mm3; archive at ftp://ftp.conagua.gob.mx/Gobmx/DisponibilidadenPresas/) and in the press-percentage frame (e.g. El Cuchillo capacity 1,123 Mm3 vs observed max 1,765.7 Mm3 including flood surcharge; the proxy threshold 441.4 Mm3 equals 39 percent of the official frame, so the proxy is the conservative/higher threshold and events under it are a subset of events under the official frame). Eligibility at T, from information available at prediction: dam has >= 365 prior daily VolumenAlm rows and VolumenAlm(T) >= threshold (onset formulation; days already below threshold are not prediction rows).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant is a fetch of CONAGUA SIH's per-dam historical CSV after its weekly update (publisher statement, SIH homepage: 'La actualizacion de los archivos se realiza semanalmente' - files are updated weekly). T is the latest Fecha row present in the file at fetch. Observed 2026-10-27: the Cuchillo, La Amistad, Falcon, Solis and Infiernillo files each carried rows through 2026-10-26 (T = yesterday); one sampled dam (LDCVC) lagged at 2026-09-28, so T is measured per dam per fetch. All feature rows are Fecha <= T; every target row is Fecha in (T, T+30]. The SIH files are the publisher's cumulative current-vintage archive, not point-in-time snapshots: the portal warns 'Algunas bases de datos pueden incluir actualizaciones retroactivos por lo que la informacion historica puede cambiar en actualizaciones posteriores', and the 1990s show partial backfill (ALRBN volume column 50 percent populated 1990-1999, 100 percent from 2000). Features are therefore stated as current-vintage values, same treatment as the published abs-visitor-arrivals-direction record; no claim of historical operational availability is made (see review). The daily observation cutoff time within a day is not documented by the publisher; the contract uses whole calendar days (America/Mexico_City), features <= T, target > T, so no intra-day ordering is assumed.
