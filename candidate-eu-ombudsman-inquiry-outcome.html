eu-ombudsman-inquiry-outcome

investigating · EU 21:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a complaint-based European Ombudsman inquiry published at opening against an EU institution or body, will the inquiry conclude with an adverse maladministration outcome (settlement code 'Maladministration found' or 'Critical remark'), vs a no-finding outcome ('No maladministration found' / 'No further inquiries justified') or a resolved outcome (solution/settled/draft-recommendation accepted), within a stated horizon after the opening instant?

What you predict

target definition

Publisher-coded settlement outcome per case (and per institution within the case), from eoCaseInstitutionSettlements.eoSettlement on the per-case record (22-value taxonomy, labels decoded from the publisher's own label store: ma='Maladministration found', crr='Critical remark', nmf='No maladministration found', nfij='No further inquiries justified', sbi='Settled by the institution', sa/spa/solution-rejected='Solution achieved/partly/rejected', sai/spai/suggestion-rejected='Suggestion(s) accepted/partly/rejected', drai/drpai='Draft recommendation accepted/partly accepted', rai/rpai/recommendation-rejected='Recommendation agreed/partly/rejected', frs='Friendly solution', dbc='Dropped by the complainant', dwc='Dealt with by a Court', casr='Closed after Special Report', oth='Other'). The primary binary label is 'adverse maladministration outcome' (ma or crr) vs all other settled codes; construction may ship the grouped 4-way variant. Label source = the settlement coding + the EODECISION document, both excludable from features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The publication instant of the inquiry's 'case opened' record (EOOPENINGSUMMARY document; the per-case record carries dateOpened, and the opening summary's documentDate). All shipped features are fixed at or before that instant: the case reference (year + handling-team initials), the ownInitiative/strategicInitiative/publicInterest flags, the institution complained against (named in the opening summary title and coded on the case), the complainant country, the topic(s), the key5 policy-area coding and the key7 'types of maladministration alleged' coding, plus the opening summary title text. The label event - the settlement code recorded at closure (dateClosed / the EODECISION document date) - always follows the instant; no feature's target window precedes it. Vintage caveat: the register serves current-vintage coding (per-case records carry dateUpdate; no historical snapshots), so features are stated as current-vintage with the cutoff recorded, the same posture as the published ec-merger-phase2-opening.
