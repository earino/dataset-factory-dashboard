What you predict

target definition

Would-be label: the site's indicator result is flagged outside the reference range (unterschritten/ueberschritten) in the next Structured Quality Report. Not measurable: the machine-readable per-hospital values sit behind the gated Qb-Datenportal XML channel; the anonymous public channel is per-hospital PDF 'Referenzberichte' (thousands per year, one per site, values embedded in prose/tables) - no bulk machine-readable distribution. IQTIG publishes only indicator definitions and aggregates (Indikatoren- und Kennzahlenuebersicht CSV/XLSX ~400 KB per Auswertungsjahr; Bundesqualitaetsbericht Excel annex; Qualitaetsreport PDFs).
