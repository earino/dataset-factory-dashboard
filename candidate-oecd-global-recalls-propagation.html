The question

what a model is asked to predict

The lead probed was the OECD GlobalRecalls portal (globalrecalls.oecd.org), the OECD Working Party on Consumer Product Safety's compilation of consumer product recalls contributed by ~47-50 national and regional authorities (launched 2012). The natural differentiated task was cross-jurisdiction propagation: at the portal publication of a product recall in jurisdiction A at instant T, will the same product be recalled in jurisdiction B within a fixed window? That is the one task shape this compilation could carry that no single national recalls portal can (in-house comparators hc-recalls-classification, openfda-food-recall-classification and rasff-notification-risk-decision are single-portal classification tasks on food/health products, not propagation). The session measured the source's coverage and closed the lead: the propagation target's negative class is censoring-dominated by heterogeneous contributor coverage, and the compilation adds no clean licence or unique access over the four reliably-contributing national portals.

What you predict

target definition

Would have been binary: a later portal record for the same product in a different jurisdiction within N days, label source = a later recall record linked to the same product. Two blockers measured/verified: (1) no recorded second-jurisdiction recall cannot be read as absence, because contributor coverage is radically heterogeneous (measured: ~20 of 50 jurisdictions carry fewer than 100 lifetime notices; the OECD's own documentation states 'The scope of the recalls depends on the mandate of the particular government authority providing the information'); (2) the 4-row sample carries no product identity field (GTIN/model) beyond product.name and GS1 GPC category facets, which identify a category, not a product - the same-product link the label depends on is not visibly present in the public search payload.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would have been the publication instant of the first-seen recall of a product in jurisdiction A (the portal's recall record date), with features from that record (product category via GS1 GPC, hazard, country of manufacture, description) - closed after aggregate measurement, before any full-schema or split work, because the target contract fails on measured coverage.
