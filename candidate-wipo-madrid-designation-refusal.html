The question

what a model is asked to predict

The lead probed was the WIPO Madrid System's international trademark registry: a new international registration (or subsequent designation) designates one or more Madrid members, and each designated office examines the mark and may issue a notification of provisional refusal within its treaty time limit, or a statement of grant of protection. The natural task was designation-level: at the Gazette publication of a new international registration, for designation (registration, member M) with notification date T, predict whether the office of M will publish a notification of provisional refusal (total or partial) within the applicable 12/18-month window from T. The task shape was attractive: a treaty-documented censoring window (Article 5(2) Agreement / 5(2)(a)-(c) Protocol; Rule 16 'possible opposition beyond the 18-month time limit' entries exist as a separate Gazette chapter), a weekly official publication carrying both the new registrations and the outcome events (refusal, confirmation, grant), large volume (~60k+ international registrations per year in recent years, each with several designations), and no known ML-ready precedent. The session closed the lead on licence grounds before any row-level probing: every free machine-readable channel for this registry carries service-specific terms that prohibit exactly what a published derived dataset is.

What you predict

target definition

Would have been binary: at least one Gazette 'Notifications of provisional refusals' entry (or 'Confirmations of total provisional refusal (Rule 18ter(3))') for (registration, member M) inside the window vs no such entry, with the no-refusal negative class bounded by the treaty window (a documented censoring boundary, which is what made the lead worth probing) - never reached. The label source would have been the refusal/grant chapters of the Gazette and the Madrid Monitor designation-status history; recorded here only so the shape of the closed lead is documented; the verdict is a licence rejection, not a task finding.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would have been the Thursday publication of the international registration in the WIPO Gazette of International Marks (weekly, official publication), with the prediction made at that publication instant using the published registration attributes (mark, Nice classes, holder, office of origin, designated members and their notification dates) - never reached: licence triage (first, cheapest) vetoed the source before schema, volume or split probing, per the standing order of work.
