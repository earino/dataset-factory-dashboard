The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the Monday publication of APHA's weekly wild-bird HPAI findings report for ISO week t, will at least one new notifiable avian influenza premises case be confirmed (case opened) in each English county during ISO week t+2? A useful prediction supports APHA-style early warning: targeting surveillance, control-zone preparedness and poultry-industry biosecurity at the county level one to two weeks ahead, using only the open weekly surveillance stream.

What you predict

target definition

Binary label per (English county, ISO week): 1 iff at least one HPAI premises case whose gov.uk case-listing 'Opened' date falls inside target week t+2 names that county. Source: the gov.uk notifiable-animal-disease case listing, entries carrying an outbreak id - current '(AIV 2026/23)' form or 2022-era '(2022/73)'/'(SOS AIV 2022/19)' form. Provenance mix measured 2026-11-06: 339 premises entries vs 1 regional zone entry (an AIPZ prevention zone, excluded) in 340 total; strain mix 334 H5N1, 1 H5N5, 4 unspecified on the old-format entries. The label source columns (opened dates, titles, counties) are excluded from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The Monday publication of APHA's weekly wild-bird findings list for ISO week t (measured update-log entries: '2 November 2026 - Updated the ... list with data for week 44 of 2026' and '26 October 2026 - Weekly update ...', both Mondays). Features are the report rows with week label <= t, plus previously published case history; the target window (ISO week t+2, Monday-Sunday) lies entirely after the prediction instant, so no part of the target precedes it and the next report's publication (week t+1) is never a feature. Where the exact publication time-of-day is needed, the instant is taken as 23:59 Europe/London of the publication day and the page's atom feed is the recorded channel for the exact timestamp (construction fetches it).
