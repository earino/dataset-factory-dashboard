The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the annual NBI snapshot, which currently Good-or-Fair highway bridges will be classified Poor at their next recorded inspection, and does design, traffic, geometry and inspection-cycle information add signal beyond the current condition rating?

What you predict

target definition

1 if the lowest numeric value among item 58 (deck), 59 (superstructure), 60 (substructure) and 62 (culvert) in the bridge's next inspection record after the snapshot is <= 4, i.e. FHWA 'Poor'; 0 if the lowest numeric value is >= 5. Records where no component carries a numeric rating are dropped, not labelled.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

June 15 of cohort year Y, the administrative date on which FHWA treats that annual NBI file as final. FHWA publishes shortly after that date and the exact publication day is not verified, so this is an administrative annual cutoff used as the decision instant, not a measured publication timestamp. Only fields from the same annual record are used.
