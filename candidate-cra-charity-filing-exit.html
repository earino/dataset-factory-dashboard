cra-charity-filing-exit

ready · CA 17:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Canadian registered charity that filed a T3010 Registered Charity Information Return for a fiscal period ending in year Y, will it file NO return for any fiscal period ending in year Y+1 (i.e., exit the filing population), given only its returns filed through year Y plus its identification attributes?

What you predict

target definition

Binary and mechanical, from the publisher's own per-year products: positive iff the charity's BN is present in the year-Y 'List of charities' Identification file and absent from the year-Y+1 Identification file. The publisher's data dictionary states 'The list only includes charities that have filed a T3010 ... for the period concerned', so absence means no filed return for any fiscal period ending in Y+1. No human judgement enters the label. Measured caveat frozen into the wording: 'absent in Y+1' is not 'never files again' - 4.73% of 2012 filers absent in 2013 reappear in 2014, so late/skipped filings are a known, measured minority of positives.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The T3010 filing deadline for the year-Y fiscal period: six months after the fiscal period end (documented on the publisher's T3010 form page: 'submit annually ... within six months of the end of its fiscal period'). At that instant everything the charity has ever filed (year-Y return and all earlier ones) is knowable, and the year-Y+1 outcome is not: returns for fiscal periods ending in Y+1 are not due until at least six months into Y+1, and the publisher's year-Y+1 list product appears later still. Features are therefore the year-Y and earlier returns only; every year-Y+1 field is the label source and never ships. MEASURED 2026-11-03 (publication lag + framing evidence, probe/out/probe_2026_11_03.json): the per-year List packages appear on open.canada.ca well after the FPE year - year-2023 package created 2025-07-29, year-2024 package created 2026-03-06 (both single-year creations that look like genuine first publication); the older packages were bulk-created (1990-2008 all 2026-02-20; 2009-2012 2021-11-29; 2013-2022 2024-12-17), so portal metadata dates do NOT evidence first publication for older years. Two honest framings therefore exist and ADVANCEMENT PICKS ONE AS ITS FIRST STEP (the cqc precedent): (a) deadline instant - the recorded instant; a retrospective filer-information scope with the explicit assumption that the year-Y return is filer-known but not yet third-party published at T (za-cct retrospective precedent, no operational third-party availability claim); (b) year-Y list publication instant - fully third-party operational (everything through FPE-Y is published), at the cost that some label events are already due by then (Jan-31-FPE charities' Y+1 returns are due before the Y-list appears), a partial-nowcast shape the abs/ocha-fts candidates already carry honestly. Label semantics are identical under both. Files carry '_updated' suffixes and packages are modified after creation (2024 package modified 2026-10-04), so labels are computed from current-vintage finalized files; no historical-vintage claim is made.
