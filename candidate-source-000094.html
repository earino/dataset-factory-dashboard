The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will this World Bank lending operation be rated Moderately Unsatisfactory or worse by IEG after it closes?

What you predict

target definition

1 if IEG Outcome Ratings is 'Moderately Unsatisfactory', 'Unsatisfactory', or 'Highly Unsatisfactory'; 0 if 'Highly Satisfactory', 'Satisfactory', or 'Moderately Satisfactory'; exclude 'Not Rated', 'Not Available', 'Not Applicable', and blank. This is the complement of IEG's own '% Outcome Satisfactory' collapse.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At Board approval, recorded as the World Bank fiscal year running 1 July to 30 June. Available then: approval fiscal year, borrower country, region, agreement type (IBRD or IDA), lending instrument (investment project financing, development policy financing or program for results), current vintage Global Practice and practice group, and the appraisal time project volume band. Not available then: IEG outcome, bank performance, quality at entry, supervision and M and E ratings, closing fiscal year, evaluation date or type, and lesson text.
