The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the instant the European Commission registers a state aid case (SA case number assigned on notification/registration, per the register's documented case fields), will the measure be handled under the ordinary/simplified procedure or will the Commission open a formal investigation under Article 108(2) TFEU before deciding?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label derived from the case's decision record: a decision opening proceedings under Article 108(2) TFEU (formal investigation) vs decisions taken without opening (no-objection, block-exemption compatibility). Label source = the case's decision fields, excludable from features. Base rate unknown until the distribution is measured.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The case's notification/registration date as published in the case record (to be verified in the JSON distribution's schema at the next probe). Features would be the fields fixed at notification: Member State, legal basis, aid instrument, sector, title text - all documented register fields; the decision-type/-date fields are post-hoc and must be excluded from features.
