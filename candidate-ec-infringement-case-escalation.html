The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an open European Commission infringement case against a Member State at a decision instant (e.g. the date a letter of formal notice is decided), will the case escalate to the next procedural stage (reasoned opinion, then referral to the Court of Justice) or be closed without escalation within a stated horizon?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label derived from the case's decision history in the register: escalation to the next stage (or a named later stage) vs closure without escalation, within a stated horizon after the instant. Label source = later decision entries for the same case number (INFR(YYYY)NNNN), excludable from features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The publication instant of the Commission's monthly infringement package / the register entry for the decision in question (to be pinned against the register's actual channel; the monthly packages are dated presscorner publications). Features would be the case's stage reached, policy area, topic, Member State, case age and the case's decision history - all published register fields; the future decisions are the label and must be excluded.
