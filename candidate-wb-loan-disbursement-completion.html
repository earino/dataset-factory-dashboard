wb-loan-disbursement-completion

dropped · US 16:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the instant the World Bank Board approves an IBRD loan (board_approval_date, a documented column: 'The date the World Bank approves the loan'), will that loan disburse at least 90% of its original principal amount over its life? (Alternative near-balanced threshold >=99% is measured and recorded; construction picks one and documents it.) A useful prediction enables disbursement-risk scoring at commitment time for portfolio management, borrower-side planning and development-finance research: roughly a third of IBRD lending historically fails to move 90% of its commitment, and that gap is the operational quantity WB cash planning and aid-delivery studies care about.

What you predict

target definition

Binary completion label: final_disbursed_ratio = disbursed_amount / original_principal_amount >= 0.90 (primary) or >= 0.99 (near-balanced alternative, base rate 51.5%), computed only on instruments whose disbursement phase is closed (terminal loan_status - Fully Repaid/Cancelled/Disbursed/Transferred, Terminated - or Repaying, or closed_date <= data-as-of; 'Repaying' with undisbursed>0 occurs on 2/9538 rows and is excluded by construction). Label source columns (disbursed_amount, undisbursed_amount, cancelled_amount, closed_date_most_recent, last_disbursement_date, loan_status, all repayment fields) are excluded from shipped features. Zero ratio (312 IBRD rows, fully cancelled/terminated with cancelled_amount = original) is a genuine negative (no disbursement ever made), distinguished from missing data by the in-row cancelled_amount. Population: IBRD instruments from DS00047 with original_principal_amount > 0, excluding guarantees (loan_number prefixes IBRDB/IBRDG, 113 rows) and B-loan/guarantee-facility types (BLNR 18, BLNC 2, GURB 84, GUBF 9); the type mix is recorded so construction can scope further with evidence.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant is the Board Approval Date, published by the World Bank at approval time (the same-day press release and projects.worldbank.org record carry it; the dataset column is documented as 'The date the World Bank approves the loan'). Features are the approval-time attributes documented by the publisher: country/economy + code, borrower, guarantor, loan_type, original_principal_amount, currency_of_commitment, project_id/project_name, board approval fiscal/calendar year. CAVEAT (documented by the publisher itself): the `region` column description reads 'Country/Economy lending is grouped into regions based on the current World Bank administrative (rather than geographic) region' - i.e. current-vintage coding, so for pre-2011 approvals region may be back-coded; the shipped task must either drop `region` or carry this as a disclosed current-vintage limitation (both options stated; construction decides, qualification enforces). All other shipped features are static attributes fixed at approval. The label resolves later, at the end of the loan's disbursement window (closed_date_most_recent / terminal loan_status), so the whole target window follows the prediction instant. Censoring is measured: approvals through 2017 are >=94% disbursement-closed by the 2026-09-30 snapshot (2010-2017: 0-6 open per approval year), so the labeled population is censored at 2017 approvals and this cutoff is recorded in the shipped rows.
