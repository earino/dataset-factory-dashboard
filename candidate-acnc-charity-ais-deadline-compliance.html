The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an Australian charity registered with the ACNC that lodged its Annual Information Statement (AIS) for the reporting period ending in year Y-1 early enough that the lodgment was already recorded 90 days before its next deadline, will the charity FAIL to lodge its period-Y AIS by the ACNC-recorded due date for that period (the AIS 'date ais received' falls after the 'ais due date', or the charity never lodges a period-Y AIS and no row exists in the period-Y AIS dataset)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label 1 = charity fails to lodge its period-Y AIS by the due date, computed from the publisher's own recorded fields in the period-Y AIS dataset: label 1 iff ('date ais received' > 'ais due date') OR (no row for the ABN in the period-Y dataset as of the snapshot). Label 0 iff 'date ais received' <= 'ais due date'. Label source columns: 'date ais received' and row presence (both excluded from shipped features; 'ais due date' is a legitimate feature because it is determined by the period end and announced deadline policy, known before T). The label matches the ACNC's own compliance KPI ('Percentage of ... Annual Information Statements submitted by the due date', Annual Report 2025-26 KPI 1.3: 76% for 2025, 73% for 2024) against measured on-time shares of 73.96% (2023 file) and 74.95% (2024 file).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Per-charity instant T = (the publisher-recorded 'ais due date' for the charity's period-Y AIS) minus 90 days. The due date is fixed by policy at six months after the reporting-period end (ACNC due-dates page), with the Commissioner's standing extension for standard 1 July-30 June periods to the following 31 January recorded in-file (measured: period ends 30/06/2023 carry due date 31/01/2024; 31/12/2024 carry 30/06/2025). Eligibility: the charity's period-(Y-1) AIS row exists with 'date ais received' <= T, and the charity is not listed as a member of an ACNC reporting group for period Y (group members lodge via their head entity; measured 2024: only 5 of 1,315 group-member ABNs also carry an individual period-2024 AIS row). All features come from the period-(Y-1) and earlier AIS datasets (finances, staffing, charity size, reporting-period structure, past lodgment timing including prior lateness) and the ACNC Register dataset; the target period-Y 'date ais received' (and row absence) is the label source and is excluded from features. Example instant (Australia/Sydney, AEDT): T = 2 November 2024 for a charity with FY ending 30/06/2024 (due 31/01/2025).
