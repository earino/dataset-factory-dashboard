The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the publication instant of complete-year T SINASC live-birth data for Brazil (America/Sao_Paulo; the publisher's own TABNET base is stamped final for year T no later than early December T+1 - measured: 'Dados finais disponíveis até 2024 - data de extração 02/12/2025'), will a municipality's cesarean share among resident live births fall by at least 2 percentage points from year T to complete calendar year T+2 - the next complete year whose every month lies after that instant? A useful prediction identifies which municipalities will move against the still-rising national cesarean level (60.6% of resident births in 2024), informing where maternal-care policy attention (SUS maternity audits, cesarean-reduction programs) is likely to matter two seasons ahead.

What you predict

target definition

Binary label for municipality m and publication instant of year T: 1 if cesarean_share(m, T+2) <= cesarean_share(m, T) - 0.02, else 0, where cesarean_share(m, Y) = count(PARTO == 2 among resident live births of m in Y) / count(all resident live births of m in Y) (denominator includes the small Ignorado stream; nationally <1% since 1996 - measured). Source columns: PARTO (dictionary: 'Tipo de parto: 1- Vaginal; 2- Cesário; 9- Ignorado'), CODMUNRES (IBGE residence municipality), DTNASC (birth date) - the label source columns are excludable from shipped features by construction (ship lagged municipality-year aggregates only). Margin 2pp is provisional and measured at three floors; the rise-direction label (>= +2pp) and a national-median-exceedance alternative are measured as calibration options; final margin fixed per split window at construction, before any scoring.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

One sentence: the prediction is made at the final-publication instant of complete-year T SINASC data (no later than early December T+1, America/Sao_Paulo), using only complete calendar years <= T as features, about the cesarean share of complete year T+2. Dated example (America/Sao_Paulo, UTC-03:00, no DST): T = 2024; feature event window = births with DTNASC in 1996-2024 (each year final per the publisher's FINAL/PRELIMINAR/PRÉVIA ladder; earliest feature events 1996-01-01); first-available timestamps and version = the 2024 CSV resource was created 2024-06-11 (preview) and the publisher's TABNET base is stamped final for 2024 at extraction 2025-12-02, the whole CSV archive being last refreshed 2026-05-08 (current-vintage; no historical vintages exist); eligibility cutoff (at the instant, from instant-visible data only): municipality had >= floor F resident live births in year T (F in {100,200,500} measured); prediction instant = 2025-12-02 (the publisher-stamped date on which complete-year 2024 data is final); target window = complete year T+2 = 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31, every month after the instant. No part of the target window precedes the instant; the 2025 preliminares (CKAN resource created 2025-10-21) and any 2026 previews are excluded from features by design.
