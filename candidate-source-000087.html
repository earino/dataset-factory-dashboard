The question

what a model is asked to predict

At a given NOAA water-level station, will the next local calendar day be a high tide flood day - that is, will verified hourly water level reach or exceed that station's NOS minor flood threshold?

What you predict

target definition

1 if the maximum verified hourly height at the station during local calendar day D is greater than or equal to the station's NOS minor flood threshold (nos_minor, station datum; equivalently converted to the MHHW datum as nos_minor minus the station's MHHW datum value), else 0. Days with fewer than 20 of 24 verified hourly values are dropped rather than labelled.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

12:00 local standard time on day D-1, using only observations timestamped at or before 11:00 LST on D-1 plus published tide predictions for day D. Lead time is 12 hours to the start of day D and up to 36 hours to its end.
