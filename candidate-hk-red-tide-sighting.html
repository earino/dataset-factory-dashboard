The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will at least one red tide sighting be reported in Hong Kong waters within the next 7 days after a stated weekly publication instant, for a monitored fish culture zone or marine station? Formulated as an occurrence onset at location-week grain (not cell-count forecasting).

What you predict

target definition

Binary: at least one red tide sighting report whose 'Date of Report' falls in (instant, instant+7] at a location within the monitored network. Label source columns 'Date of Report' and 'Location' in the AFCD red tide CSV, excluded from features by the window construction. Censoring is material: 'no report' conflates absence of a bloom with absence of observation. AFCD documents a monitoring programme (weekly sampling at 6 core fish culture zones, fortnightly at 5 more plus 5 offshore stations, quarterly at the remaining 15 fish culture zones) but publishes no per-day monitoring log, so the eligible negative population cannot be enumerated from the source.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Weekly. Candidate instant: the publication of the red tide update containing all reports dated <= D, conservatively placed at D+8 23:59 HKT, with features restricted to data published by that instant and the target window starting strictly after it. NOT SUPPORTED AS WRITTEN: the publication lag is not bounded by observed evidence - the 2025-04-02 Wayback vintage carried its latest record at 2025-03-25 (8 days), while the 2019-11-02 vintage carried its latest record at 2019-05-31 (155 days).
