The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the Thursday the application's pre-grant publication appears, will that application be granted a U.S. patent within four years of its publication date?

What you predict

target definition

y = 1 if the application's own pgpub_id appears in pg_granted_pgpubs_crosswalk with current_patent_id_flag = TRUE and the joined g_patent.patent_date (earliest, if several patent_ids) is on or before published_date + 4 years; y = 0 otherwise (no grant, or a grant dated after the window). Absence of a crosswalk row is a true negative for this horizon because the window for every cohort closes by 2019-12-31.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The pre-grant publication date (published_date), typically 18 months after earliest filing. Every shipped feature is a field of that publication record, a date on or before it, or a classification documented as at submission; office actions, examiner decisions, allowance, grant, patent_date and current_patent_id_flag are not features.
