ready · EU 03:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an organisation listed as registered in the EU Transparency Register at the instant of a semi-annual register snapshot (the export timestamp stamped inside the snapshot file), will it still be listed in the next semi-annual snapshot, or will it have been removed from the register by then?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label: identificationCode of the organisation is ABSENT from the next semi-annual snapshot's export (removed from the register) vs PRESENT. Label source is presence in a different file (snapshot t+1), which is excludable from features by construction. Removal mixes automatic removal for a missed compulsory annual update (documented rule: deadline one year after registration and then one year after each annual update; missed deadline -> suspended, then 'automatically removed two weeks after the deadline'), voluntary deregistration, and rare enforcement removals; the publisher's own mapping report counts '87% complying timely ... and 1 496 registrants removed' for its reporting period. The label is state-based, not absorbing: a small measured share (92-200 per measured window, some of it validation-lagged new registrations) reappears in later snapshots; the label stays 'absent at t+1' regardless of later reactivation.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The export instant of semi-annual snapshot t, i.e. the <exportDate> timestamp carried inside the snapshot XML (observed exports 2015-01-21T19:30 CET ... 2026-06-30T20:00 UTC; the publisher states the organisation lists are 'published twice a year' and the live register lists are 'updated daily'). Features are the organisation's fields as published in snapshot t only (identity, registration category, country, declared goals/interests, members and FTEs, declared financial data for the closed financial year, EP-accredited-person count, networking, EU legislative proposals listed, lastUpdateDate as at t). The target window is (t, t+1] where t+1 is the next snapshot's export instant, strictly after the prediction instant.
