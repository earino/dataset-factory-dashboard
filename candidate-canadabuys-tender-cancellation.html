investigating · CA 20:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Government of Canada competitive solicitation (noticeType in the RFP-like set: Request for Proposal, RFP against Supply Arrangement, Request for Standing Offer, Request for Supply Arrangement, Invitation to Qualify) whose original tender notice is published on CanadaBuys at instant T (publicationDate of the original notice row), will the solicitation be cancelled before award - i.e. its terminal tenderStatus is 'Cancelled' rather than 'Expired'? Features are the original-notice fields fixed at T: title and description text, contracting entity and its city/province, GSIN/UNSPSC and descriptions, procurement category, procurement method, selection criteria, trade agreements, regions of opportunity and delivery, expected contract start/end dates, and the closing date (documented UTC-0500) which with publicationDate gives the posting duration.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: 1 if the solicitation's terminal tenderStatus (current archive) is 'Cancelled'; 0 if 'Expired'. Label source column: tenderStatus-appelOffresStatut-eng/-fra, excluded from shipped features. Population restricted to the RFP-like notice types above; excluded: Request for Information (measured 3.2% award-notice rate - no award by design), Advance Contract Award Notice (award pre-decided), Directed Contract and Not Applicable (non-competitive instruments). Observed absence vs no-event: 'Expired' is an observed terminal state (closed without cancellation), not missing data. Multi-row rule: 499/6,829 FY2023-24 solicitations carry amendment rows with their own later publication dates, and 144 of those show differing statuses across rows - construction defines precedence as any-row-Cancelled (solicitation-level label), verified on a bounded sample against the notice pages; measured FY2023-24 impact of the precedence choice is small (base rate 6.08% under max-amendment-row vs 6.1-6.9% band). Censoring: solicitations still Open at harvest are excluded. Optional recorded variant (NOT the primary label): no award notice published among Expired RFP-like solicitations (measured ~48% in the FY2023-24 + FY2024-25 award files) - ambiguous because award-notice publication is not guaranteed for every award.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant is the original tender notice's publicationDate (the row with amendmentNumber 000; amendment rows are separate later publication events and are excluded from features and from the instant). Every shipped feature is a field of the notice as published at T, each documented in the publisher's own XML data dictionary (fetched 2026-10-31, 60,478 bytes). The label is a later state change of the same notice: the publisher's data dictionary defines tenderStatus as 'A status that indicates whether a tender is Open (ie: accepting bids), Expired (no longer accepting bids) or Cancelled', and the publisher's support page states the files 'will include information on new, open, cancelled and expired notices that are published on the CanadaBuys website' and that 'Tender notice closing times will be displayed in Universal Time Coordinated minus 5 hours (UTC-0500)'. As-of policy: the open-data files re-state the CURRENT status of each notice (the complete file carries live Open rows; the closed-FY files carry only terminal statuses - measured FY2023-24 file: 6,414 Expired / 415 Cancelled, zero Open). Historical cancellation DATES are not published in the bulk files, so labels are read as terminal states from an archive snapshot pinned by sha256 at harvest; still-Open solicitations are censored (excluded, not imputed). Cancellation is operationally observable in real time on the CanadaBuys site and the files refresh at least daily (2026-2027 tender file Last-Modified measured Sat, 31 Oct 2026 10:30:31 GMT). Residual limitation, recorded not resolved: rare late reversions of already-published statuses cannot be excluded for the most recent window; construction runs a bounded notice-page verification on a sample of Cancelled rows.
