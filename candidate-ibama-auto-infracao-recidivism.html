The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the instant IBAMA issues a new environmental infraction notice (auto de infracao, lavratura) against an entity, will the same entity (CPF/CNPJ) receive another IBAMA infraction notice within the following 12 months?

What you predict

target definition

Binary label: exists another auto de infracao in the same published table with identical CPF_CNPJ_INFRATOR and DAT_HORA_AUTO_INFRACAO in (T, T+365 days], where T is the current auto's lavratura instant. Label source columns: CPF_CNPJ_INFRATOR and DAT_HORA_AUTO_INFRACAO; both excludable from shipped features (entity keys shipped hashed). Censoring: right-censoring at the last observed lavratura date in the extract; entities leaving enforcement attention are treated as no re-autuacao observed (this is a re-AUTUACAO task, not a reoffense task - enforcement effort confound stated in the task card, not hidden).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The prediction instant is the lavratura instant: DAT_HORA_AUTO_INFRACAO of the current auto de infracao, the earliest documented moment the enforcement act exists (recorded by the electronic AI-e form since 2019-10-08, and by the launch date DT_LANCAMENTO for the pre-2019 analogue SICAFI series). Features are restricted to (a) the current auto's issuance-time attributes (infraction type/code, proposed sanction TIPO_AUTO, initial value VAL_AUTO_INFRACAO, gravity classification GRAVIDADE_INFRACAO, conduct MOTIVACAO_CONDUTA, UF/municipio, bioma, action motivation TIPO_ACAO, operation name) and (b) the entity's PRIOR autos only (DAT_HORA_AUTO_INFRACAO strictly earlier than the instant). Post-instant process events defined by IN Ibama 19/2023 (ciencia of the auto, defense, julgamento in first instance ~30 days from file receipt, appeal ~20 days, second instance, Cadin inclusion) are label-window events of the current auto's lifecycle, never features. LIMITATION, recorded: the daily export is current-vintage only (no publisher archive of past exports); prior-history features are therefore current-vintage rows with per-row DT_ULT_ALTERACAO / TP_ULT_ALTERACAO / JUSTIFICATIVA_ALTERACAO available to quantify how much history was revised. The 12-month target window follows the prediction instant entirely.
