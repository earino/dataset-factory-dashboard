The question

what a model is asked to predict

For each City of Cape Town switching substation, at 06:00 SAST on day D, predict whether that substation's evening peak apparent-power load (maximum half-hourly MVA reading over 16:00-22:00 SAST on day D) will exceed the trailing-90-evening 95th percentile of its own evening peaks (evenings ending strictly before the instant).

What you predict

target definition

Binary: evening-peak exceedance. Label = (max half-hourly MVA over 16:00-22:00 SAST on day D for substation S) > (95th percentile of the same substation's evening peaks over the trailing 90 evenings ending strictly before the instant, minimum 30 prior evenings for eligibility). Label source columns: the target day's 16:00-22:00 MVA readings, plus the same substation's prior evening peaks; these are sealed (private label matrix) and never shipped in any solver-visible feature frame. The baseline operand (trailing p95) is computed from feature-side readings <= the instant and may ship as a numeric feature; the target evening readings cannot.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

06:00 Africa/Johannesburg (SAST, UTC+2) on day D, within the artifact's history. Features are the substation's half-hourly MVA readings with timestamps <= the instant (including day D's 00:00-06:00 readings), trailing aggregates computed only from readings <= the instant, plus calendar fields and substation identity. The evening target window 16:00-22:00 SAST lies entirely after the instant. RETROSPECTIVE USE: the publisher released the entire 2022-01-01..2024-12-31 window as one closed table (item created 2026-02-28, last modified 2026-03-02, hasStaticData), so this is a retrospective_research task per docs/PREDICTION-CONTRACT.md: no historical-operational-availability claim is made, label timing is unverified at any historical instant outside the artifact's own timestamp order.
