anm-sigbm-emergency-transition

investigating · BR 09:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the publication instant of ANM's monthly dam-safety bulletin for month M (published in the first days of month M+1, reporting every registered dam's Nivel de Emergencia as of 01/(M+1)), predict for each dam enrolled in the PNSB whether its emergency status (Nivel de Alerta / Emergencia 1-3 vs Sem emergencia) will CHANGE during calendar month M+2, using registry attributes (construction method incl. upstream a montante, material, height, volume, mineral, UF, population downstream), the dam's own published level history from prior bulletins, and current CRI/DPA classification. A useful prediction supports ANM's remote-inspection prioritization (fiscalizacao remota, the bulletin's own producing unit) and civil-defence readiness before a dam's emergency declaration changes, the moment agency resources get reallocated.

What you predict

target definition

Binary, per dam: the dam's emergency status in bulletin M+2's per-dam level lists (state as of 01/(M+3)) differs from its state at 01/(M+2) as recorded in bulletin M+1 - i.e. any change (entry into Alerta/Emergencia, escalation, or exit) during calendar month M+2. Variants to measure at construction: entry-or-escalation only; Alerta-or-Emergencia entry from Sem emergencia only. Label sources are the bulletin level lists and the bulletin 'Barragens com situacao de alerta ou emergencia alterada' transition tables; both are excludable from the shipped features. Population: dams enrolled in the PNSB (461 of 908 in the current registry; all 77 dams currently in Alerta/Emergencia are PNSB dams - measured).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Publication of the monthly Boletim Mensal de Seguranca de Barragens de Mineracao for reference month M. Each bulletin states its reference window explicitly ('Os dados analisados neste boletim referem-se as atualizacoes ocorridas entre 01/10/2026 e 01/11/2026') and is published 4-7 days after the window closes (archive page: Outubro 2026 published 05/11/2026 12h45; Setembro 2026 published 06/10/2026). At the instant the predictor knows: the full per-dam level lists as of 01/(M+1) (bulletin tables 5-8), the month-M transition table (bulletin table 4: dams with level changed during M, previous -> current, no per-dam event dates), and registry attributes. The target month M+2 opens at 01/(M+2), strictly after the instant; the M+1 intermediate month is skipped because its transition table is not yet published and its table cannot be split into before/after-instant events (no dated events) - see notes.md 'timing' for the dated example. Timezone: America/Sao_Paulo (federal publications carry no timezone; declared Brazilia time).
