The question

what a model is asked to predict

Given a newly located earthquake, will at least one aftershock at least one magnitude unit smaller than the mainshock (with a magnitude floor) occur within 100 km and 72 hours?

What you predict

target definition

Positive (1) if the catalog contains at least one earthquake with a different event id, magnitude >= max(4.5, mag_mainshock - 1.0), hypocentral/epicentral distance <= 100 km from the mainshock epicenter, and origin time strictly after the mainshock origin time and <= mainshock origin time + 72 hours; otherwise 0. The mainshock itself is excluded by id, and the label window opens at origin time + 1 second.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The mainshock origin time (UTC), using the event's own location, magnitude, and solution-quality metadata as recorded in the current USGS PDE snapshot. Historical first-automatic availability is unverified; later revisions may be present.
