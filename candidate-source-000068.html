The question

what a model is asked to predict

When a U.S. certificated carrier starts scheduled passenger nonstop service on a domestic city market pair it has not served in the previous 12 months, will that service still be operating in the anniversary quarter 12-14 months later?

What you predict

target definition

label = 1 if the carrier's summed DepPerformed (departures performed) on the directed city-market pair over months M+12, M+13 and M+14 equals 0, i.e. the route is not operating at its one-year anniversary; label = 0 if any departures were performed in that three-month anniversary window.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Ninety days after the end of entry month M, after the monthly T-100 compilation for M is assumed available. Only T-100 records for months at or before M may enter features; months after M are unseen.
