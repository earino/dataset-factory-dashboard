The question

what a model is asked to predict

At the instant a WTO member's Request for Consultations is circulated with a DS number - the moment a formal dispute enters the WTO dispute settlement system - will that dispute escalate to first-instance litigation (the Dispute Settlement Body establishes a panel for that DS number within the eligibility window), or will it end without one (settled in consultations, withdrawn, or lapsed)? The prospective user is a trade ministry or mission to the WTO in Geneva weighing litigation cost and coalition formation at filing time, and the research question the bench cares about: do the agreements cited, the measure/subject, and the pair's dispute history carry signal beyond complainant-respondent identity? The identity-prior risk is real (the afdb/wb-loan family defect) and is exactly what the pre-registered triad tests.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the DSB establishes a panel for this DS number within the eligibility window (consultations request at least 24 months before the artifact cutoff - measured: 97.1% of establishments occur within 24 months of consultations, median 155 days, n=273). Label source columns (all post-instant stage dates) are excluded from every shipped CSV. No external threshold is involved (the event is the publisher's own procedural milestone), so the known-event requirement is met by named disputes with their published outcomes: DS600 Malaysia v EU palm oil (consultations 15 Jan 2021, panel established 28 May 2021) and DS620/DS631 (2023/2024 filings, no establishment yet - the censoring behaviour working as expected). Right-censoring: consultations inside the 24-month eligibility tail are excluded and counted, never labelled negative. Settlements are mostly NOT formally notified (the dates file carries one MAS date; the stage file counts 156 'mutually agreed solutions, lapse of authority' events across the whole era), so the negative class means 'no panel established by cutoff', a documented meaning, not 'settled'.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The circulation date of the Request for Consultations for the DS number (all WTO dates are Geneva-local circulation dates, published the same day to all members). Features available at that instant: complainant(s), respondent(s), the WTO agreements and provisions cited (the request itself cites them and is circulated at the instant), the measure/subject short title, the calendar, and trailing strictly-prior dispute history for the complainant-respondent pair. Every later stage (request for panel establishment, DSB establishment, panel composition, report, adoption) is outcome-side and excluded from features.
