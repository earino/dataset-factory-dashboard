The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an asylum corridor (country of origin O, country of asylum H) whose total government-procedure asylum applications FIRST reach at least 500 in year T0 (its entry year), will the corridor's total applications over the two following years (T0+1 and T0+2) reach at least twice the T0 total? One row per corridor, taken once at first crossing; a useful prediction enables two-year reception and processing-capacity planning for newly-arrived flows. Measured fallback design (also one-shot, decided before any score): will the crossing year's flow grow by more than 25% in T0+1.

What you predict

target definition

Binary label: (corridor G/P application total in T0+1 + total in T0+2) >= 2 x corridor G/P total in T0. Source column: Number of Applications summed over Government-procedure (Procedure Type G), person-count (Application Data Type P) rows per corridor-year (the sum rule validated against BAMF in the parent record). The T0+1 and T0+2 values are the label operands and never ship as features. Observed absence: corridor row missing while the host still reports that year = 0; host silent in an outcome year = unobserved target, row excluded (19 rows measured). Eligibility (first year >= 500) is decided only from the at-instant year-T0 row - no whole-series future inclusion. Fallback label: total(T0+1) >= 1.25 x total(T0).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Publication-lag nowcast at the annual instant: the first refresh of the UNHCR HDX global package (data.humdata.org, dataset unhcr-population-data-for-world) that carries the entry year T0's data (observed cadence: refreshes 2026-06-11/19/22 and 2026-08-20T15:33:49Z; the 2026-08-20 file carries years 2000-2025). Features are the corridor's published yearly totals for years <= T0 only. The outcome years T0+1/T0+2 are unpublished and unavailable on this channel at the instant (first published ~mid T0+2/T0+3); for historical rows the outcome window has partially elapsed at the instant - no 'window follows the instant' claim is made (the abs-visitor-arrivals-direction / unhcr-refugee-data-finder posture). Corridors entering 2025 are live strict forecasts: their whole outcome window 2026-2027 follows the 2026-08-20 instant. Dated example in notes.md (AFG->USA).
