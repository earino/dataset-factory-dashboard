The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a substance identifiable in a documented EU inventory (e.g. the EC Inventory / REACH-registered substances) at a stated instant, will it be identified as an SVHC and included in the REACH Candidate List (published by ECHA under Article 59(10) REACH) within the next window (e.g. 12 months or until the next scheduled update)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: substance included in the Candidate List in the window vs not. Label source: the Candidate List itself (per-entry inclusion date and reason for listing, published on the ECHA website), which would be excluded from shipped features. Positive events: 219+ substances/groups since 2008 per ECHA annual-report figures quoted in searches (read 2026-11-03); clustered by update tranche.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Candidate design (not constructed): a fixed instant before each Candidate List update (updates are irregular, historically ~2 per year; ECHA's own prioritisation document says 'The Candidate List is generally updated twice a year (normally in June/July and in ...)'), with features restricted to regulatory state observable at that instant: harmonised CLP classification (Annex VI as amended by dated ATPs), Annex XIV / Annex XVII / POPs entries (all dated EU legal acts on EUR-Lex), and static identifiers (EC/CAS numbers from the EC Inventory, published in the OJ).
