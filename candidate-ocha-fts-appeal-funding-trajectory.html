The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a UN-coordinated humanitarian appeal or response plan (HRP/CAP/flash appeal/HNRP/SRP and similar single-country plans) at its public launch instant (requirements first published - GHO early December for the following year, or the plan's own release, whichever the record dates), will the plan receive funding equal to at least 50% of its ORIGINAL (launch-vintage) requirements by the close of its plan year? Features are launch-time attributes (original requirements, plan type, GHO membership, country/region, emergency linkage) plus strictly-prior-year history for the same country (prior-year plan coverage, funding levels, series length). A useful prediction identifies, months ahead, which appeals will end the year underfunded - the quantity GHO gap analysis reports after the fact - enabling donor advocacy and fundraising prioritization.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the plan's FTS funding total for its plan year (usage year = plan year; commitments + paid contributions, pledges excluded, USD, publisher's own attribution including boundary flows) divided by its ORIGINAL requirements (launch vintage) is >= 0.50 -> 1, else 0. Label source: per-plan-year funding totals (FTS flow endpoint incoming.fundingTotal with planId+year filter; equal to the publisher's HDX distribution on single-country rows - measured: Yemen HRP 2023 1,774,327,599, Afghanistan HRP 2023 1,678,347,238, both API==CSV to the dollar) and origRequirements from the plan endpoint; both excludable from features by construction (features carry launch attributes and strictly-prior years only). Censoring: plan-years whose year has not closed are excluded and counted (2026 live edge: 24 primary rows), never imputed. Zero-funding rows are the publisher's recorded outcome (empty funding fields in the HDX CSV counted at construction, not dropped silently). Population: PRIMARY = single-country plan-years with origRequirements > 0, 2000-2025 (782 rows; 60 multi-country regional plans excluded from the primary task because the HDX CSV splits them into country slices whose funding sum covers only ~78% of the plan total - measured on Burundi RRP 2021: slices 14,978,233 vs API plan total 19,282,778; regional plans can be a secondary population with flow-endpoint totals if construction wants them). Appeal-linked funding only; the country rows without plan linkage (2,678 HDX CSV rows, 'Not specified' plans) are outside the task population, so the label's meaning is single-population throughout.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The plan's public launch: the instant its requirements are first published (GHO publication in early December for the following plan year, or the plan's own release for late-launched plans). Dated example (all UTC): Afghanistan HRP 2023 (plan id 1117) - GHO 2023 launched 2022-12-01 with Afghanistan at $4.6B; the HRP itself launched end of December 2022 / early January 2023; prediction instant pinned at 2023-01-01T00:00Z. Every feature is fixed by that instant: original requirements (plan endpoint origRequirements = 4,627,363,573, matching the December 2022 GHO ask), plan type, country, and strictly-prior plan-years (all closed years, final values). The target window is the plan year 2023-01-01..2023-12-31, strictly after the instant. VINTAGE POLICY (the decisive timing finding): the HDX CSV 'requirements' column is CURRENT-vintage - of 836 plans with requirements, 286 were revised up and 166 down after launch, and the CSV shows the revised value in revised cases - so it must never be used as a launch feature; the plan endpoint's origRequirements is the launch-vintage denominator, verified against the December 2022 GHO on the Afghanistan fixture. Prior-year funding totals used as features are current-vintage final values for closed years, approximating what was knowable at launch (late-reported flows are the measured gap - flow records carry firstReportedDate and decisionDate; 94.5% decisionDate coverage in a 200-flow sample); this retrospective approximation is declared as the restricted protocol and probed at gate 1.5, not assumed to be operational forecasting.
