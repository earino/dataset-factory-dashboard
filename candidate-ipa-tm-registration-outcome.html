ipa-tm-registration-outcome

investigating · AU 20:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a trade mark application arriving at IP Australia on its application date (the filing date for direct Australian applications; the date it seeks protection in Australia for Madrid designations), will it ever achieve registration on the Australian Trade Marks Register, rather than ending before registration (refused, ex parte/inter partes refused, rejected, withdrawn, lapsed, not filed, cancelled, voided, or linked into another application)?

What you predict

target definition

Label: whether the application ever achieves registration - a standing (is_standing=TRUE) registered event in the trade-mark events table, cross-checked against the application table's gained_registration_status_date and current status. Retroactively expunged registrations (is_standing=FALSE - revocation that legally 'unhappened' the grant, per the dictionary's 'What is is_standing?' section) do NOT count as registered. Post-registration lifecycle (ceased/removed/expired after grant, non-use removal) does not change a positive label. Negative: application terminated before any registration (refused/rejected/withdrawn/lapsed pre-registration/not_filed/cancelled/voided/linked). Censored: applications with no registration and no pre-grant terminal event at the extract date (recent filings, or rare very slow prosecutions) - excluded by eligibility cutoff, never silently dropped. Label source columns (events, status, registration dates) are excluded from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The application_date instant as defined by the publisher: 'The date at which the application arrived for processing to the IP Australia system... the filing or lodgment date of a direct application' (or the date an international right seeks protection in Australia). Everything used as a feature is on the application as lodged: IP right sub-type, Nice classes and goods/services scope from the application, mark-type flags and the presence of a word phrase, applicant metadata (organisation vs individual, country, state, postcode, ABN match) from the founding applicant, agent presence, filing channel (direct vs Madrid designation, convention priority, divisional parent), and derived applicant history computed strictly as-of the application date. Nothing observed after lodgement ships as a feature: examination events, adverse reports, acceptance, opposition, publication, status, gained_registration_status_date, gained_enforceable_status_date and deemed_retired_date are all post-instant by construction.
