The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will a charity newly entered on the Register of Charities for England and Wales be removed from the register within five years of its registration date?

What you predict

target definition

y = 1 if date_of_removal is non-null and is on or before the fifth anniversary of date_of_registration; y = 0 if there is no removal on or before that anniversary, including charities still registered or removed later.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

date_of_registration, the day the charity is entered on the register. Only attributes present in the current daily extract are treated as registration-time descriptors; classification, area, governing-document and objects fields are not time-stamped, so their as-of-registration availability is assumed rather than verified.
